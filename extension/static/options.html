Unyap

Impatience, now a browser extension.

No key yet? Get a key from OpenAI and set a monthly budget for it in your OpenAI dashboard, so spending stays capped. The key is stored only in this browser and sent only to api.openai.com. A new video costs about $0.001; videos you already analyzed are free.

Off: videos you already analyzed show at once, and new ones wait for you to click "Unyap this video". On: every new video with captions is analyzed as soon as it opens.

Show live Decisions API activity and judgment progress over the video.

Analyses are cached in this browser and removed after 60 days.

Unyap runs on your computer with your own key, at your own responsibility and cost. Nothing is sent to me, its developer, and I store no data. Terms of use · Privacy policy
